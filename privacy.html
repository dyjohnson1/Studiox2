Privacy Policy

Last updated June 2026

Xavier Haughton Studios ("the Studio," "we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

When you make an inquiry, place an order, or join our mailing list, we may collect your name, email address, shipping address, and order details. When you browse the site, we may collect basic analytics data such as pages visited and general location.

How We Use It

  • To respond to inquiries and fulfill orders
  • To communicate about works, exhibitions, and editions you have expressed interest in
  • To improve the site and understand how it is used

Payment Information

Payments are processed securely by our payment partners (including Shopify, Apple Pay, and PayPal). The Studio does not store your full card details.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers necessary to operate the Studio — such as shipping carriers and payment processors.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by writing to studio@xavierhaughtonstudios.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be directed to the Studio at the email above.
